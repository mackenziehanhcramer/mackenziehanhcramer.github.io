Publications


Measuring the Stories in Contemporary Songs


David Bamman, Sabrina Baur, Anna Ho, Tom McEnaney, Mackenzie Cramer

We analyze the degree of narrativity present throughout popular and prestigious English song lyrics across 1960-2024. Anthology of Computers and the Humanities.


Multimodal Conversation Structure Understanding


Kent K. Chang, Anna Ho, Ti Ti Nguyen, Yilin Yuan, David Bamman, Mackenzie Cramer

We introduce a suite of tasks focused on conversational role attribution (speaker, addressees, side-participants) and conversation threading (utterance linking and clustering), drawing on conversation analysis and sociolinguistics. arXiv preprint.


Speak, Memory: An Archaeology of Books Known to ChatGPT/GPT-4


Kent K. Chang, Sandeep Soni, David Bamman, Mackenzie Cramer

In this work, we carry out a data archaeology to infer books that are known to ChatGPT and GPT-4 using a name cloze membership inference query. arXiv preprint.
